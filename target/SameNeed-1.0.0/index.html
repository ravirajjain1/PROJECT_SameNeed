How SameNeed Works

Four straightforward steps that turn single high-cost repairs into affordable group bookings.

1

Post Your Need

Specify the service category, describe your problem, and define your locality and preferred schedule.

2

Form a Group

Neighbours in your locality join your request. Your identity is kept private with anonymous aliases.

3

Receive & Negotiate

Verified local technicians bid on your group. Use group chat and counter-offers to reach the best price.

4

Confirm & Save

Accept the offer together, get the work completed at your agreed time, and pay the discounted group rate.

Built for Community Trust

Designed from the ground up to protect user privacy and guarantee service quality.

🛡️

Privacy Protected Chat

Every member in a group receives an anonymous alias (e.g. Member 02, Group Creator). Personal phone numbers and details remain hidden until bookings are confirmed.

✅

Vetted Service Professionals

Contractors and technicians must verify business documentation, maintain minimum rating thresholds, and adhere to community service standards.

⚖️

Transparent Bidding & Voting

All members see quotes, offer expirations, and negotiation rounds in real time. Offers can only be accepted when the group consensus is reached.
